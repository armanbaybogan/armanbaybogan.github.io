Setup and troubleshooting for codicile Watch & Mac Voice.

readiepeasy@gmail.com

Setup

  1. Download the Mac companion and open it on the Mac you want to use.
  2. Open Codex on the Mac and sign in to your supported ChatGPT account.
  3. Open codicile on the Mac, review the data-sharing disclosure, complete remote-access setup, and keep the Mac awake and online.
  4. On iPhone, use the camera to scan the pairing code shown by codicile on the Mac.
  5. On Apple Watch, review the data-sharing disclosure and allow microphone access when asked.
  6. Start or stop the voice session from the Watch.

Before contacting support

Check that the Mac is awake, Codex is installed and signed in, codicile reports remote access as connected, the Watch has microphone permission, and Tailscale is connected. Available actions depend on your Codex setup and permissions.

If the Mac companion asks you to finish active work and restart Codex during first-time desktop handoff, follow that prompt only after saving or completing the active work.

Diagnostics

Use codicile’s diagnostic export only when support asks for it. Review the export before sharing it and remove personal information. Do not send pairing codes, tokens, passwords, private conversation text, or raw audio by email.

What to include

Tell us which step failed, whether the Mac companion reports account and network readiness, your device models, and the app versions. A short description is usually more useful than a full conversation transcript.
