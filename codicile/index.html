How it works

A small bridge to the work you already have.

codicile keeps the conversation close while the Mac remains the place where your Codex work runs.

01

Start on Mac

Install the companion, sign in to your supported ChatGPT account through Codex, and keep the Mac awake and online.

02

Pair your Watch

Use the iPhone camera to scan the pairing code shown by codicile on the Mac, then review the Watch microphone disclosure.

03

Keep moving

Use the Watch for the voice interaction and return to Codex on the Mac when you need the full desktop view.

Requirements: an awake, online Apple silicon Mac running macOS 15 or later with a compatible Codex installation and signed-in account, an iPhone for setup, an Apple Watch with microphone access, and a Tailscale connection. Network and account terms are provided by those services.
