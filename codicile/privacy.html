Privacy

Effective September 19, 2026 · Published by Arman Bayboğan

codicile is a companion for Apple Watch, iPhone, and Mac. It connects your Watch to your own Mac so you can use your existing Codex setup. codicile does not operate a cloud conversation server.

Information processed

When you use voice, codicile processes microphone audio, assistant audio, and the conversation or task context required for the requested interaction. The Mac sends this information to OpenAI using the ChatGPT account signed in to your Codex setup. Tailscale provides the configured network path.

iPhone camera access is used to scan a pairing code. Apple Watch microphone access is used for the voice session. Pairing credentials are stored in Apple Keychain on the Mac. The apps keep the local settings needed for pairing and operation.

Diagnostics and local control

codicile can create a bounded diagnostic export when you choose it. The export contains state, counters, error information, and app or system version details needed for troubleshooting. It is not intended to contain microphone PCM, credentials, raw conversation text, or network secrets. Review an export before sharing it.

You can stop a session and revoke local pairing data through the available Mac controls. Data held in your OpenAI account is controlled through your OpenAI account and its applicable settings and policies.

Service providers

Voice and task context are sent to OpenAI as required by your Codex and ChatGPT setup. Remote traffic uses your configured Tailscale path. Apple provides operating-system services such as Keychain, WatchConnectivity, camera, and audio routing. codicile does not sell data, show advertising, or use third-party cross-app tracking.

Requirements and limitations

Use requires an awake, online Mac with a compatible Codex installation and signed-in account, an iPhone for setup, an Apple Watch with microphone access, and a Tailscale connection. Available actions depend on your Codex setup and permissions. A first-time desktop handoff may ask you to finish active work and restart Codex; save or complete active work before doing so.

Children and changes

codicile is not directed to children. This policy may change when the app or its data practices change. A new effective date will be shown with an updated policy.
